## COLD vs. WARM TRAFFIC RULES

**Cold traffic** = Never been to the site before. Came from content.
- CTA: Free Reset only. No pricing shown. No tier menu.
- Landing page: reset.html
- No mention of $27/mo until they opt in.

**Warm traffic** = Visited before. Retargeting. Clicked a link. Already on the list.
- CTA: Join BTH Rise Monthly. Price front and center.
- Landing page: tier-3.html (renamed/repositioned as BTH Rise Monthly)
- Popup at 30 seconds: 10% off first month with code RISE10.

## AVATAR TARGETING

**Avatar 1 — Pickup Grinder (Primary)**
Age 20–28. Plays 3–4x/week. Body always wrecked. No system.
- Hook: "You're not working harder than everyone else. You're working without a system."
- Offer angle: "BTH Rise gives you the structure gym + court."
- Content: Heavy workload → recovery → system → results stories

**Avatar 2 — Returning Hooper (Secondary)**
Age 24–32. Coming back from injury/life. Hesitant, stiff, breaking down.
- Hook: "Every time you feel right, something goes wrong. That's not bad luck."
- Offer angle: "Start with Foundation Month. Your first month rebuilds the body that keeps breaking."
- Content: Return stories, "I almost gave up on pickup" angles

**Messaging priority:** Lead with Avatar 1 in cold content. Lead with Avatar 2 in paid retargeting.
