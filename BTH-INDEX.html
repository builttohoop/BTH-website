The Problem

Most hoopers aren't broken. Their program is.

You can get stronger and still feel slower. You can rest and still never feel ready. You can play through it and keep ending up back at zero. That's the cycle. BTH is built to break it.

01

Too random

Rehab drills, heavy lifting, pickup, and jump work mixed together with no progression. The body never gets a clean signal to improve.

02

Too much ego

Chasing hard sessions before you can control positions, absorb force, or repeat quality movement. More intensity on a broken foundation just breaks you faster.

03

No transfer

The BTH Method

Restore. Rebuild. Rise.

A clear 3-phase structure that takes you from broken down to performance-ready — in order, for a reason.

R1

Restore

Clean up the basics. Improve positioning, control, foot and ankle function, hip behavior, and core stability — so movement stops fighting itself before you add any load.

R2

Rebuild

Layer in structured strength, unilateral work, tendon loading, and force production without losing movement quality or the confidence you just earned in Phase 1.

R3

Rise

Convert the foundation into speed, bounce, rhythm, and court-ready performance — so the body feels athletic again, not just managed. This is where the game comes back.
