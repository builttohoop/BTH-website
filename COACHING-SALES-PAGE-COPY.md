## FAQ

**How is coaching different from the membership?**
The membership is the full system, built for hoopers like you, that you run on your own. Coaching is built for *you* specifically — your programming, your film, your schedule — with direct access to Ty.

**What does "async access" mean?**
You reach Ty by chat/DM through your block — send film, ask questions, get adjustments — instead of waiting on a weekly slot. Plus one scheduled live call each month.

**Why are spots capped?**
Because real coaching takes real time. 5 for 1:1, 12 for the group. When it's full, it's full.

**What happens to my membership after coaching?**
It stays. 12 months free after 1:1, 3 months free after group — then it continues at a discounted $19/mo for as long as you keep it.

**Do I need the app?**
No. Coaching is run directly — chat, shared docs, film, and your live call. Your membership unlocks the app on top of that.

**Can I cancel group coaching?**
Yes — group is month-to-month. You keep everything you've already downloaded.

**What if I need to stop the 1:1 block?**
Before your build is delivered, you get a **full refund** — no questions. Once your custom programming and schedule are built and coaching starts, the block is non-refundable, because the work is already done and it's yours to keep. *(Policy locked by Ty 2026-07-05.)*
