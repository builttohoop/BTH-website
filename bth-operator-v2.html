Install on Your Phone
iPhone (Safari Only)
1
Open this file in Safari
Must be Safari — Chrome won't work for install.
2
Tap the Share button
The box with the arrow pointing up at the bottom.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down the share sheet if you don't see it.
4
Name it "BTH" and tap Add
It now lives on your home screen like a native app.
Android (Chrome)
1
Open in Chrome
Download the file, tap to open in Chrome.
2
Tap the 3-dot menu (top right)
Then tap "Add to Home Screen".
3
Tap Add
Done. BTH lives on your home screen.
