How it moves

Four steps. No games.

  1. Apply. Three minutes. No card, no commitment.
  2. Consult call. You hear back within 2 business days to set it up.
  3. Straight answer. The right path and the real numbers — or an honest “not yet” and where to start instead.
  4. The build. Your 12 weeks get built around your body, your week, and your game. Then the work starts.
Where you are decides the path

The area logic.

  • Within 30 miles of the BTH Atlanta-area home base — travel’s included. No add-on.
  • 31–40 miles — Hybrid works, plus $35 per in-person visit.
  • 41–50 miles — Hybrid works, plus $65 per in-person visit; a far-hybrid setup (one in-person + one live video session a week) is on the menu in this band.
  • Past 50 miles — Online 1:1 is the path: the same full backbone, coached remote.

Shared-session trips split one travel fee across the group — nobody pays it twice. Your ZIP places the band; that’s all it’s used for.

Why an application

Five spots is five spots.

Real coaching takes real hours, so the roster is capped at five active clients — on purpose. The application isn’t a hoop to jump through; it’s how Ty makes sure every spot goes to someone the work will actually move.
