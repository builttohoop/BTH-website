The method

Rebuild. Reload. Return.

Twelve weeks, three phases, one build — the same arc on either path. Every phase has a job, and every week reports to it.

Weeks 1–4

Rebuild

Set the base. Movement quality, mobility, strength and conditioning your body can actually tolerate right now, recovery habits, and a daily-life foundation the rest of the block stands on.

Weeks 5–8

Reload

Load the base. Usable single-leg strength, acceleration and deceleration, landing, change of direction, power, and basketball conditioning — capacity you can spend on the court.

Weeks 9–12

Return

Take it back to the court. Progressive basketball workload, higher-speed movement, fatigue tolerance, real court exposure — and your continuation plan, so week 13 isn't a cliff.

Location logic

Where you are sets the path.

Hybrid runs on route days out of the BTH operating base. The bands are simple and public:

0–30 miles
Included. Travel and gym access are handled in the price.
31–40 miles
+$35 per in-person visit.
41–50 miles
+$65 per in-person visit. A split week — one in-person session, one live video session — is on the table in this band; the call settles it.
Beyond 50 miles
Online 1:1 — the same system, delivered remote.

Distances are one-way road miles from the BTH operating base. On a shared trip, one travel charge covers the trip and is split among the hoopers in the room — you never stack surcharges.

Questions

Straight answers.

What happened to the old 1:1 and Group Coaching?

This replaced them. One flagship — Return-to-Hoop Coaching — with two ways in. Shared sessions still exist, but inside Hybrid Premium as part of your block: a small room of 2–4 compatible hoopers, never a separate cheaper tier, and everyone in it still runs their own individual program.

Which path is mine?

The application asks where you are and which path you'd prefer; the call confirms both. Within 30 miles, Hybrid is fully included. 31–50 miles adds a per-visit travel charge ($35 or $65 by band). Past 50 miles, it's Online 1:1 — same system, delivered remote.

Why an application instead of a checkout button?

Because there are 5 active spots total and the fit has to be real — both ways. On the call you hear the price, the terms, and the plan stated plainly, and nothing is charged until you decide. If it's not a fit, we'll point you to the right BTH step instead.

What do shared Hybrid sessions actually mean for me?

At least 6 of your 12 sessions are private — Weeks 1 and 12 always are. When a session runs shared, it's 2–4 hoopers whose training genuinely lines up, and you get a private 30-minute remote review within 72 hours on top. Your programming never becomes a group template.

What happens to my membership after the 12 weeks?

Stay Ready is included the whole time you're coached, then runs 3 more months free after you finish. After that it continues only if you opt in — $19/mo, locked below the standard $27. Nothing renews on its own.

What if I need to stop mid-block?

The exit ladder above is the policy: 7 business days full refund, full refund until your build is delivered, weeks 1–4 exit at 50%, week 5+ pause or transfer. On Online, a month that isn't delivered isn't charged. All of it is in the written agreement before any payment.

Is this medical care?

No. BTH Return-to-Hoop Coaching is performance coaching. It is not physical therapy, medical care, or treatment for any condition. For injuries or persistent pain, see a physician — BTH builds performance alongside what your clinician clears.

Do I need a gym?

On Hybrid, gym access is handled — it's included within 30 miles. On Online, your program is built around the equipment and space you actually have.
