TODAY'S WORKOUT — Knee + Quad Reset

Spanish Squat

3 × 10 · 3 sec hold at bottom

Strap or post hold, heels down, load the quads in the deepest position. This is direct patellar tendon work.

VMO Lunge

3 × 8 each leg

Front foot elevated slightly, drop the back knee slow. You'll feel the inner quad firing.

Posterior Chain Bridge (single leg)

2 × 12 each leg

One leg glute bridge. Drive through heel. This balances the quad dominance most hoopers have.
