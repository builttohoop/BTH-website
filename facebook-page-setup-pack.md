## 13. Domain Verification (Critical for iOS / Privacy Sandbox)

Without this, Apple users mostly drop out of your retargeting audience. Worth 15 minutes.

1. Meta Business Suite → Brand Safety → **Domains** → Add → enter `built-to-hoop.com`
2. Choose **Meta Tag Verification**
3. Copy the meta tag they give you (looks like `<meta name="facebook-domain-verification" content="abc123..." />`)
4. Send it to me here in chat — I'll add it to the `<head>` of every HTML file in one shot.
5. Click **Verify Domain** in Meta. Done.
