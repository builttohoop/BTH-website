The BTH Difference

Playing and Training
at the Same Time.

Most programs make you choose. BTH is built around pickup — not separate from it. The readiness framework is the unique mechanism — nobody else in the market does this.

Light
Casual runs, minimal jumping. Train legs again after 24 hours.
Moderate
Full games, regular jumping. Wait 36–48 hours before lower body training.
Hard
Lots of sprinting and jumping. Wait 48–72 hours before training legs again.
"Ran hard yesterday? Your body already told you. Here's what to do with that information."
This is the core of BTH — a readiness-based system that integrates with your game instead of competing with it. Train smarter. Stay available. Play longer.
