The Fix

Build Around the Joint.

The Knee Protection Track is a 6-week structured load progression. No mystery exercises. No "100 squats a day." Just the right inputs in the right order so the structure around your knee can take what pickup hands it.

01
Quiet the Joint
Two-week deload phase. Drop the load that's beating it up. Reintroduce isometrics. Stop the cycle without stopping training.

The biggest knee bypass surgery you can do for free — get the hip absorbing force again. RDLs, single-leg work, controlled tempo.

04

Now you can load. Step-ups, split squats, controlled depth. The joint gets stronger because the system around it stopped overworking it.
