FAQ

Common Questions.

Is this ankle rehab?

No. BTH is basketball training — strength, stability and movement work for hoopers. Rehab for an injury belongs with a professional; BTH is for building the base once you are cleared to train.

I am scared to cut hard again. Will this help?

Confidence tends to come back through stacked small wins — sessions where the ankle does its job and you notice. That is what the Reset and the Ankle Rebuild Track are built to do.

How do I know if I am ready to start?

If you are cleared for normal activity and training, the first days are low-intensity and scale to you. If you are not sure, ask the professional who saw the injury — then start.
