The Fix

Range. Then Control.
Then Strength in Range.

The Hip Reset Track is a 6-week structured progression. Not a 30-minute mobility flow. Not a yoga sequence. A loaded hip rebuild built specifically for hoopers coming back to pickup.

01
Open the Joint
Two-week mobility primer. End-range positions, controlled breathing, isolated movement before any load. Get the joint moving before you ask it to do work.
02
Own the Range
Active range work. Anti-rotation. Hip CARs. The work that turns "I can stretch into it" into "I can move into it on demand."
03
Load It
Loaded RDLs, single-leg work, lateral squats, controlled tempo. The hip gets stronger in the position pickup demands.
04
Express It
Bring it onto the court. Cuts, deceleration, first-step work. The mobility you built becomes the mobility you can use at speed.

Real Questions

Real Answers.

I just need to stretch more, right?
No. Stretching adds passive range. Pickup needs active range — the hip has to do work in those positions, fast. The Hip Reset is built around loaded range, not passive stretching.
I'm coming back from a long layoff. Where do I start?
If your hips are the bottleneck, this. If your whole base feels off, Tier 1 Foundation. If you're not sure, run the free 5-day reset first — Day 1 will tell you a lot.
Can I run it alongside another program?
Yes. The Hip Reset Track is designed to slot into a normal training week. 3 short sessions per week, 25–35 min each.
What if my knee or back is the bigger problem?
Hip work usually helps both — but if pain is sharp, look at the Knee Protection Track too, or grab Tier 3 for everything.
