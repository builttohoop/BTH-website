How to choose

Start with the level your body has actually earned.

If you are still dealing with stiffness, recurring discomfort, loss of confidence, or inconsistent readiness, Tier 1 is where you start. Do not skip the base because your ego wants the harder plan.

What BTH is not

This is not random basketball conditioning.

BTH is not made to smoke you for content. It is made to move you forward with a system. That means better decision-making, better progression, and better transfer to the court.
