Day 01 of 05
Hip Reset
Open what pickup locks.
GoalRESET
Time15–20 MIN

Open your hips, reduce stiffness, and give your body a foundation worth building on. Move slow on every rep — this is not a conditioning workout. No pain. If something pinches, back off immediately. This is about control and position, not effort.

Reset BlockBreathing + hip mobility first
01
90/90 Hip Lift Breathing
2–3 × 4 breaths / side

Exhale fully at the bottom. Let the hip melt. No forcing range.

02
Adductor Rockbacks
2 × 6 each side

Slow and controlled. Stop at the first sign of pinching.

03
Glute Bridge ISO Hold
2 × 20 sec

Drive through both heels. Squeeze at the top. Breathe through it.

Control BlockStability and hip loading
04
Assisted Split Squat
2 × 5 each leg

Use a wall or rack for support. Stay tall. Front leg working, not collapsing.

05
Standing Hip Shift
2 × 6 each side

Slow lateral shift. Feel the hip loading. No rushing — the glute catches the pelvis.

What you should feel
  • Less stiffness in the hips
  • More control through range
  • Smoother, freer movement
Focus  Move slow — every single rep. No pain. Back off if it pinches.
