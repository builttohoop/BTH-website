Reset Questions.

Is this really free?
Yes. Five days of emails. No trial, no hidden charge. At the end I'll tell you about Tier 1 Foundation ($19) if you want the full system — but only if you want it.
How long do the workouts take?
10–20 minutes a day. Day 4 is the longest (~22 min). Days 1–3 can be done in your living room without a gym.
Can I do this if I already have pain?
The Reset is built for the gray-zone stuff — tightness, heaviness, minor stuff that never goes away. If you've got sharp pain or a recent injury, see a PT first. Then come back.
I'm 30+ and don't feel like I used to. Does this help?
That's exactly who it's built for. The 5-Day Reset is the starting point for Avatar 2 — the Returning Hooper — who played all through high school/college and is now trying to get the body to hold up for pickup and leagues.
