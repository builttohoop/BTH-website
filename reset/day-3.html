Day 2 got your ankles under you. Today the hips and ankles have to work together — slow, and under control.

Day 3 — Movement Control
CONTROL  ·  20–25 MIN  ·  Where the hips and ankles start talking.

Improve control, coordination, and pain-free movement so your body moves efficiently on the court. Hip and ankle work is done — now those two start talking to each other. Movement control is the part most hoopers skip; they go straight from stiff to heavy lifts and it never transfers, because they never taught the body to move with control first.

Reset Block — Carry over from Days 1 + 2
90/90 Hip Lift4 breaths
Exhale fully. Reset your breathing pattern.
Ankle Rocks2 × 8 each
Slow drive. Knee tracks over the toe.
Glute Bridge Hold2 × 20 sec
Squeeze at the top. Breathe through it.
Movement Block — Direction changes with control
Controlled Jog → Stop → Change Direction3–5 reps
Take your time. No rushing the change. Decelerate — don’t crash.
Control Block — Stability under load
Dead Bugs3 × 8 each side
Low back stays flat the whole time. Slow and deliberate. Breathe on the way down.
Step-Ups (Controlled)3 × 6 each leg
Drive through the heel. Don’t push off the back foot. 3 counts up, 3 counts down.
Split Squat Hold3 × 20 sec each leg
Stay tall. Front knee tracks the toe. Breathe.
Single-Leg Balance Reach2 × 6 each side
Slow reach in each direction. Control the return. Don’t rush.

What you should feel:

  • More coordination in movement
  • Better balance under control
  • Smoother direction changes

Focus: Move controlled — every rep. Stay balanced. No rushed reps, ever.
