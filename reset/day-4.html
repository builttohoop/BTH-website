Day 4 — Strength That Moves
STRENGTH  ·  25–30 MIN  ·  Strength that leaves the gym with you.

Build strength that supports movement, not just size. Most gym programs build strength that stays in the gym. Movement-based strength trains through basketball-relevant positions — unilateral, controlled, functional. Every rep today should feel strong but smooth. No grinding through force. This is the foundation that makes Day 5 possible.

Strength Block — Lower body load
Goblet Squats3 × 8
Chest up. Knees track the toes. Sit into it. Full depth with control.
DB Romanian Deadlift3 × 8
Hinge at the hips. Slight knee bend. Feel the hamstrings. Back stays neutral.
Split Squats2 × 6 each leg
Stay tall. Front heel stays down. Control the descent — 3 sec down.
Core + Support Block — Stability for movement
Dead Bugs2 × 8 each side
Low back flat the whole time. Slow and deliberate. Never rush.
Side Plank2 × 20 sec each
Hips stacked. Don’t let them sag. Breathe steady.
Control Block — Single-leg work to finish
Step-Ups (Slow)2 × 6 each leg
3 counts up, 3 counts down. No push-off from the back leg. The heel drives.
Single-Leg Balance2 × 20 sec each
Slight knee bend. Hold perfectly still. Breathe.

What you should feel:

  • Strong but not stiff
  • More stable on one leg
  • Movements feel controlled

Focus: Move strong, stay smooth. No forced or grinding reps. Control over load, always.
