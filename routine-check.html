The 5-Minute Check

A wall, a chair, your phone. Five items. Then send it back.

Before you start. Barefoot. Prop the phone on a shelf or a stack of books, about knee height, six to eight feet back. Film each item so you can watch it back — you'll only send me the ones that don't come back clean.

First, three questions

These outrank everything else on this page. Answer them honestly before you move. They aren't movement findings — they're a reason to have somebody look at you in person.
Pain that wakes you at night, or pain at rest?
Any numbness, tingling, or a leg that gives way?
Any joint that locks or catches mid-movement?

What comes back is your nightly routine — twelve to fifteen minutes, run it every night before bed. After four weeks, run this check again and send it back, and I'll write the next one off what moved.

Assessment and training, not diagnosis. Nothing here treats, cures or diagnoses any condition. Pain that persists deserves a clinician's eyes — please go see one.
