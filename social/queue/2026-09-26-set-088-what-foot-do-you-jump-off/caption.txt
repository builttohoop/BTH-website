What foot do you jump off for layups — and does the other one even get asked?
Right-hand layup, left foot — you know the rule. But the footwork tends to follow the leg you trust. A layup is a one-leg jump, and if one side is less sure of that load, the body routes every layup through the other. Train takeoffs and landings on both legs, the less trusted side first.
