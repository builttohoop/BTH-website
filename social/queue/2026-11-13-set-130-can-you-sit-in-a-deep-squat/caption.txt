Can you sit in a deep squat for thirty seconds?
Stand near a wall or something sturdy to grab. Feet flat, hips below your knees. Stop if anything hurts. If the heels come up, the ankles may not be bending far enough, and the knees, hips and back tend to find the range instead. It's a quick look, not a diagnosis.
