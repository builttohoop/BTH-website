How It Works

Weeks 1-4.
One Job.

Foundation is the first of three four-week blocks. This is the part most people skip, and it is the part that makes the next two work. One job per block, and this one is build strength.

Week 1

Reload

Ease in. Clean reps, a little under your ceiling. Learn the sessions. The point of week one is to start well, not to impress anyone.

Week 2

Build

The most work of the block. Same weights, full volume.

Week 3

Express

Fewer reps, more intent. Move the bar and the jumps fast. Go heavier only if every set was clean.

Week 4

Primer

A planned pull-back. Cut the volume and stop further from failure, so you walk into Rise fresh instead of flat.

Next up: Rise, weeks 5-8. Same rhythm, new job.

Do I need a gym?
A standard gym covers it: dumbbells, a bench, a barbell and a box for the jumps. Nothing fancy required.
What if I'm dealing with pain right now?
Foundation is training, not treatment. If pain is sharp, persistent or getting worse, or you have numbness or swelling, see a doctor or physical therapist before you train. For a dull, day-after ache, check the Hip, Knee, or Ankle tracks.
