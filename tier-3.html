Questions

Real Answers.

Do I need Tier 1 or Tier 2 first?
Nope. Tier 3 unlocks everything. If you know you want the full system, Tier 3 is the play from day one. Most hoopers who want the whole thing skip straight here.
What if I already bought Tier 1 or Tier 2?
Email tyrell@built-to-hoop.com with your Gumroad receipt — you get a credit toward Tier 3 for what you've already paid.
What's Q&A look like?
Batched, not live. Submit your question whenever. I answer in rolling batches — weekly or biweekly depending on volume. You get real responses, just not real-time. Think asynchronous coaching.
What if I only want one add-on?
Go to the add-ons page — each one is available standalone. Tier 3 makes sense if you want two or more, or if you want the main programs too.
Monthly or annual — which do I pick?
If you want to test it, monthly. If you know you'll use it for a year or more, annual saves $127 — basically 7 months free.
If I cancel, do I lose everything?
You keep the programs you downloaded as long as they're yours. Member-only content (monthly updates, Q&A, member-only drops) stops when your membership ends.
What happens when new tracks or programs drop?
They get added to your library automatically. No upgrade fee, no extra charge. That's the whole point of the membership.
